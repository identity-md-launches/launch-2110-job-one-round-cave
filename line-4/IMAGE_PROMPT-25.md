Flat earth pigments only, no gradients, no shadows, no modern rendering. Center this small simple wide pictograph with ample white margins, no lettering, numbers, symbols, signatures, watermarks, border or frame, no hands, no frog (the existing wall already has Pepe). Aspect of painted motif approximately 3:1. This is the small new addition representing retaining two compatible choices while changing one.

and five digits on each of two hands. No new hand or stencil was generated.
